Artist: Pietro Nobile
Song: Manime

The idea was made to convert Pietro Nobile execution of his composition Manime first to a Gran Coda timbre, then to Morphoné. He composed Manimé somehow willing to experiment "the piano language on guitar", therefore he was more than happy to join this project.

Strategy: only a portion of 2'.48" of the whole song was converted. The straight recording of the execution, summed in mono, with no other treatments, has been split into three parts, treated separately with Basic Pitch, gradually testing several different settings, to obtain their relative .mid files.  These have been edited and assembled with Logic Pro, then used to drive Gran Coda Multisample from PSound - https://www.psound.it/gran_coda.html.

The reverb used, wich is part of the artist's emotional and technical language, has been directly added in his Studio by the him on all the final versions presented here.

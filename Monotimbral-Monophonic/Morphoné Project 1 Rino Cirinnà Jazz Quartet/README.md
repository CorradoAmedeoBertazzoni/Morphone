Rino Cirinnà Jazz Quartet.

Luca Biggio sax
Rino Cirinna sax
Angelo Cultreri organ
Andrea Liotta drums

Song: Lawns by Carla Bley

The original stems of the two solo performances of Rino Cirinnà and Luca Biggio, recorded @ 44.1/16, untreated, have been spearated in smaller phrases. Then Timbre Transfer towards trumpet 
models have been performed in several ways, with several different settings of the two jpnb included here and of the basic tools of Google Magenta Tone Transfer: https://g.co/magenta

The various results, per segment, have been evaluated to obtain the most satisfactory results, then the final result has been assembled using Logic Pro as DAW.
